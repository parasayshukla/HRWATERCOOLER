About Us

Leading the Path to
Comfort & Safety

Welcome to HR Watercooler, a pioneer and trusted name in commercial cooling solutions since 1999. Proudly manufacturing under our flagship brand, HR Water Cooler, we specialize in the design and production of premium Stainless Steel (SS) water coolers.

With over two decades of industry experience, we have built a solid legacy founded on engineering excellence, product durability, and unmatched cooling performance. Our SS water coolers are meticulously crafted for schools, hospitals, corporate offices, industrial factories, and public spaces.

At HR Watercooler, we don't just manufacture cooling equipment — we engineer reliable hydration solutions that stand the test of time.

Our Vision
To be the most trusted and innovative leader in the refrigeration and water-cooling industry, universally recognized for quality, sustainability, and community well-being.
Our Mission
To manufacture and deliver high-quality, energy-efficient, hygienic SS water coolers providing accessible hydration solutions to all.
Sales Network

Why HR Watercooler

Why Choose Us?

When you choose an HR Watercooler, you are investing in quality, reliability, and peace of mind. Here is what sets Himalay Refrigeration apart:

Decades of Expertise

Established in 1999, we bring over 25 years of proven manufacturing experience and technical know-how to every cooler we build.

Premium Grade Stainless Steel

We use high-quality, food-grade Stainless Steel for both the inner tanks and outer bodies, ensuring maximum hygiene, rust resistance, and a long lifespan.

Energy Efficient Performance

Our water coolers are equipped with heavy-duty, advanced compressors that deliver rapid cooling while keeping your electricity consumption low.

Robust & Heavy-Duty Design

Built tough to withstand continuous use in demanding Indian weather conditions and high-footfall areas.

Dedicated Customer Support

We believe our relationship begins — not ends — at the point of sale. We provide prompt after-sales service and easy availability of spare parts.

Eco-Friendly Technology

Environment-friendly non-CFC R-134a refrigerant and HFC Puf foaming technology that ensures safety and better environmental performance.

Innovation

Our Technology

Advanced engineering and cutting-edge technology at the core of every HR Watercooler we manufacture.

Sensor Based Watercooler

Sensor based touchless technology for safety of use and contactless dispensing at all times — hygienic and modern.

Condenser Coil Technology

Anti-corrosive grooved copper tubing and aluminium blue fin technology providing faster cooling and energy efficiency.

Eco-Friendly Refrigerant

Environment-friendly non-CFC R-134a refrigerant provides safety and better performance with zero ozone depletion.

HFC Foaming Technology

Climate-friendly HFC Puf technology that eliminates the traditional refrigeration system and ensures environment safety.

Technical Specifications

  • Stainless Steel 304 Grade — inner tank & outer body
  • Tecumseh / Emerson make reciprocating compressor
  • Adjustable auto control switch
  • Refrigerant: R-134A / R-22 (Non-CFC)
  • Aircooled copper condenser
  • Power supply: 200–230V / 50Hz
  • Water inlet hosepipe & overflow pipes included
  • Specially designed food-grade plastic float valves
  • Adjustable pedestals — highly non-corrosive strong material
